What Do These Metrics Mean?

Domain Authority (DA)

Domain Authority is a search engine ranking score developed by Moz that predicts how likely a domain is to rank in search results.

Page Authority (PA)

Page Authority estimates the ranking strength of an individual webpage based on its link profile and other signals.

Spam Score (SS)

Spam Score is an SEO risk indicator. A higher percentage may indicate characteristics associated with potentially spammy sites.
